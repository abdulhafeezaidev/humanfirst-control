# HumanFirst Ethics Charter

HumanFirst is built on enforcement, not surveillance.

## We Do NOT:
- Read content
- Record screens
- Log keystrokes
- Spy on users
- Accuse students using AI models

## We Do:
- Enforce access boundaries
- Apply time-based restrictions
- Provide transparency to users
- Respect institutional policies

Ethics are non-negotiable and part of the product design.
